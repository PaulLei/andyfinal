**Neurologic Solutions Strengthens Leadership Team with Hiring of Dr. Mark Hays and Dr. Golnoosh Kamali to Drive Product Innovation and Site Engagement**

**BALTIMORE, MD – September 2024** – Neurologic Solutions, Inc., an innovative medtech digital health startup developing advanced neurodiagnostic software, today announced the appointment of two key leadership positions: **Mark Hays, PhD**, as Director of Product Development, and **Golnoosh Kamali, PhD**, as Director of Site Engagement.

These strategic hires come at a pivotal time as Neurologic Solutions accelerates the development, clinical site integration, and commercialization of EpiScalp™, its proprietary decision-support software designed to transform how neurologists diagnose epilepsy from electroencephalograms (EEGs).

“As we advance EpiScalp™ toward regulatory clearance and clinical adoption, bringing on proven talent with deep domain expertise in computational neuroscience, clinical workflows, and medtech commercialization is critical,” said Andy Gotshalk, CEO of Neurologic Solutions. “Mark and Golnoosh bring an extraordinary combination of scientific rigor, clinical immersion, and technology translation from Johns Hopkins University. Their leadership will ensure our software not only pushes the boundaries of neurodiagnostics but integrates effortlessly into routine hospital workflows.”

**Dr. Mark Hays – Director of Product Development**

In his role as Director of Product Development, Dr. Mark Hays will lead the engineering and translation of Neurologic Solutions’ computational algorithms into intuitive, enterprise-grade software applications.

Dr. Hays holds a B.S. in Biomedical Engineering from Brown University and a Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering from Johns Hopkins University, where he was advised by renowned epileptologists Dr. Nathan Crone and Dr. Joon Kang. His doctoral research specialized in validating computational markers for seizure localization in epilepsy patients undergoing intracranial EEG (iEEG) monitoring. Working directly with patients in the Epilepsy Monitoring Unit (EMU), Dr. Hays regularly conducted neural recordings and brain stimulations, establishing key insights on delineating epileptogenic networks and predicting surgical outcomes—work that authored 9 peer-reviewed journal articles and 13 conference proceedings.

He completed a Postdoctoral Fellowship at Johns Hopkins University, applying brain stimulation techniques to improve the diagnostic validity of passive iEEG language mapping for surgical considerations. Hays's rare blend of hands-on EMU clinical workflow experience and advanced engineering expertise enables him to bridge the gap between technical software features and the practical needs of diagnosing neurologists.

**Dr. Golnoosh Kamali – Director of Site Engagement**

As Director of Site Engagement, Dr. Golnoosh Kamali will drive partner hospital relationships, clinical site onboarding, and commercial strategy to ensure high-impact implementation of Neurologic Solutions’ platform.

Dr. Kamali earned her Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Johns Hopkins University under the advisement of Dr. Sridevi Sarma, specializing in mathematical and computational modeling of complex dynamical systems from human iEEGs. Most recently, Dr. Kamali served on the Technology Development Team at Johns Hopkins Technology Ventures (JHTV), where she spearheaded market research, commercialization strategies, and commercial support processes for healthcare software innovations and startups.

During her tenure at JHTV, Dr. Kamali served as technical product lead in the startup generator program Hexcite, supported early-stage digital health innovations through the National Science Foundation (NSF) I-Corps program, and managed SaaS platforms for clinical trial management. Her combined background in complex systems modeling and medtech venture commercialization equips her to effectively bridge technical development, site operations, and clinical partnership growth.
